# 2026 portrait-reference research

Researched 2026-10-01 for a female model aged approximately 20–24, photographed at Clear Water Bay beaches or in the Tai Hang Tun country-park grassland. This is an editorial selection for the shoot, not a platform-wide popularity ranking.

The research compared 2026 social and publication reporting, public photobook previews, interviews, photographer credits, age evidence and Fujifilm's camera manual. Selected images were downloaded from the public promotional versions actually displayed by the source pages and visually inspected. The eight selected files, source URLs and checksums are recorded in [image-sources.json](image-sources.json).

## Exclusions and attribution checks

- Removed the previous child/family portraits, Lennon/Yoko album cover, flower still life, empty seascapes, historical pool-party imagery and unrelated classic works. Photographer prestige alone does not make them relevant to this model and these locations.
- Reviewed [広瀬すず《レジャー・トレジャー》 and 奥山由之](https://mdpr.jp/news/detail/2855238), plus [the 2022 interview](https://mdpr.jp/interview/2997718). The verified previews inspected were primarily interiors and a swimming pool; they were not forced into a beach/country-park selection.
- Reviewed [森七菜《WANDERLUST》](https://mdpr.jp/news/4211100). The geographic and individual-image age requirements were not established sufficiently for inclusion.
- Reviewed [今田美桜《ラストショット》's 2025 reprint](https://mdpr.jp/news/detail/4668075), which confirms a Thailand shoot at age 22 in 2019. The inspected public image was an interior/glass portrait, so it was excluded. No incorrect Philippines location was used.
- Photographer attributions were read from the actual image captions. In particular, *まっさら* is credited to 菊地泰久 in the verified source. Article-interview portraits, which may be made by a different photographer, were not substituted for album photographs.
- Only public promotional/official images are used. No paywall was bypassed and no complete photobook was copied. Eight selected image files retain the source-served bytes and existing watermarks. Credit and source links accompany each image group; the uncredited Instagram offshot is identified separately.

## X-T5 technical checks

| Item | Official source | Result reflected in the guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dynamic range | [Image-quality settings](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-t5/menu_shooting/image_quality_setting/#dynamic_range) | DR200 requires ISO 250 or higher; DR400 requires ISO 500 or higher. Suggested scene recipes respect these limits. |
| Standard ISO range | [Sensitivity](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-t5/taking_photo/iso/) | Standard sensitivity begins at ISO 125. Extended low ISO is not recommended as a blanket bright-sun fix. |
| Portrait AF | [AF/MF settings](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-t5/menu_shooting/af_mf_setting/) | Face/eye detection is distinct from animal/vehicle subject detection. Enabling face detection turns the other subject-detection setting off. Use the near eye, with single-point fallback when detection fails. |
| Metering with detection | [Metering](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-t5/taking_photo/photometry/) | Manually selected metering modes take effect only with face/eye and subject detection off. The field guide prioritizes histogram/highlight review instead of promising spot metering on the face while eye detection is active. |
| Burst mode | [Continuous shooting](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-t5/taking_photo/continuous/) | Short continuous bursts support movement prompts; the guide starts with CL and avoids unnecessary maximum-speed bursts. |

23mm and 75mm give approximately 35mm and 112.5mm full-frame equivalent angles of view on the X-T5. Camera-to-model distances, apertures, shutter speeds, WB and JPEG simulations are practical starting points for this shoot, not the source photographers' EXIF or official reproductions of their film stocks. Scene presets use M + Auto ISO so the selected shutter speed remains under the photographer's control. IBIS does not freeze a moving person; f/1.2 is an option for controlled still portraits rather than the default for every frame. The X-T5 has no built-in flash, and flash effects are not promised with the user's listed equipment.
