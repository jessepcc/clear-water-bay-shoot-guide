# 清水湾女生外拍现场指南

Live at https://clearwater-bay-shoot-guide.vercel.app/clear-water-bay/.

A mobile-first, static Chinese-language field guide for Clear Water Bay beaches and the Tai Hang Tun country-park grassland, prepared for a 2026-10-03 shoot with the Fujifilm X-T5, Fujinon XF 23mm F1.4 and Viltrox AF 75mm F1.2.

The guide includes five scene presets, eight verified public reference images from six photobooks, location and posing prompts, and a sourced 2026 social-media trend section. Original reference-image bytes and sample watermarks are preserved. Publication years, photographer credits and official behind-the-scenes material are identified separately.

Research sources and selection limits are documented in [research-notes.md](research-notes.md). Each reference image's source page, credit and checksum is recorded in [image-sources.json](image-sources.json).
